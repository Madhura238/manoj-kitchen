About Us

A kitchen built with love, not a business plan

Manoj Kitchen didn't start as a business—it started with a passion for cooking. My father has always loved preparing delicious home-cooked meals for our family, friends, and guests. Whenever he cooked, everyone would say, "We wish we could have this food more often!" Seeing people enjoy his cooking has always been his biggest happiness."

It all began with a few food reels on Instagram. Friends and relatives remembered his cooking and started asking if they could order meals too. That's how Manoj Kitchen was born—a small home kitchen where every meal is cooked fresh with care, just like it's made for our own family.Thank you for supporting our dream. We hope every meal brings you the warmth and comfort of ghar ka khaana—from our home to your table. ❤️
